अयोध्या दीपोत्सवAyodhya Deepotsav

After fourteen years of exile and victory over Ravana, Shri Ram, Sita and Lakshman are returning home on the Pushpak Vimana. On this moonless Amavasya night, the people of Ayodhya light rows of diyas along the Sarayu so the Vimana can find its way. Light the ghats, guard the flames from wind and mischievous vanars, and make this the brightest Deepotsav ever.
